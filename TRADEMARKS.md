# Trademark Policy

"Code60" and the Code60 logo are trademarks of Code Manufactory LLC. This policy
explains how you may use them. It covers the names and logos only; the code
itself is licensed separately under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE), which
does not grant trademark rights (section 6).

The goal is simple: people should always be able to tell whether software or
a service comes from the Code60 project.

## Allowed without asking

- Using "Code60" to refer to the project accurately, for example in articles,
  tutorials, talks, reviews, and comparisons.
- Saying that your software works with, integrates with, or is based on
  Code60, as long as it does not suggest endorsement or official status.
- Distributing unmodified copies of official Code60 releases under the
  Code60 name.
- Using the name in the name of a non-commercial user group or meetup,
  provided it is clearly identified as community-run.

## Requires permission

- Naming a fork, modified version, product, or service "Code60" or any name
  confusingly similar to it. Forks are welcome; please give yours a distinct
  name and logo.
- Offering commercial support, training, hosting, or consulting described as
  "official", "certified", or otherwise endorsed by the Code60 project.
- Using the Code60 logo, or a modified version of it, in marketing for a
  product or service.
- Using "Code60" in a company name, product name, or domain name.

## Describing a fork or related work

A fork or derivative may state its origin accurately, for example:
"Based on Code60" or "A fork of Code60". Remove the Code60 logo and change the
application name, window title, and CLI command name in distributed builds.

## Requesting permission or reporting misuse

Open an issue in the project's issue tracker.

This policy may be updated as the project grows.
