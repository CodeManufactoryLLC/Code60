## Normative Language (RFC 2119)

All project governance documents MUST adhere to [RFC 2119](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc2119).

The key words "MUST", "MUST NOT", "REQUIRED", "SHALL", "SHALL NOT", "SHOULD", "SHOULD NOT", "RECOMMENDED", "MAY", and "OPTIONAL" in this document and in all other project governance documents are to be interpreted as described in RFC 2119.
